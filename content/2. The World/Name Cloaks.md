#Lore #Amethyst_Academy 

When Wizards who study magic at [[the Amethyst Academy]] graduate from their base studies and choose a subclass they undergo a magical ceremony to protect their name and shield them from divination or influence that hinges on a name. This protection is the Name Cloak.

The Wizard chooses a word to become their name, and by tradition almost all graduating Wizards choose a word that begins with the letter A. Prominent examples are Acerbic, Angular, Amethyst, and Arch. The exceptions to this tradition are Wizards who don’t continue their studies at the Academy—voluntarily or otherwise.

Anyone who hasn’t received the Wizard’s name in writing from the Wizard themself, or from someone else who was gifted the name in that manner, forgets it entirely. Even those who hear the name spoken aloud after the cloaking hear the Name Cloak instead—only those who have been gifted or discovered the name hear it for what it is. For spells and effects that hinge on knowing or speaking a target’s name, the Name Cloak offers no benefit—only the name itself confers power.

Once a creature has been gifted a Wizard’s name, whether before or after the Name Cloak was created, that knowledge cannot be rescinded except by Modify Memory, Wish, or similar magic. A carefully worded Wish spell can also determine a Wizard’s name.
