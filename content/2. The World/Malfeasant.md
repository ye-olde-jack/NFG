#Lore #Mageborn 

Those who were expelled from the Amethyst Academy for some trespass, real or imagined, or perhaps were never taken in by them and learned magic on their own. Common folk are often mistrustful of spellcasters who do not wear the Amethyst Academy colors, so they may need to hide your identity and abilities, especially from the Amethyst Academy and the Knights of the Silver Order.
