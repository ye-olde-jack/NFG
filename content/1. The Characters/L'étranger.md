#NPCs #Spirit 

Also known as, 'the Man in Black', 'the Stranger', 'the Pilgrim under Stars', and 'the King of Night'.

Of the [[Spirits]], there are few captured in whispered words around campfires and in tattered tomes which could be considered 'Great Spirits'. Particularly ancient and powerful spirits that can represent fundamental parts of nature.

Forged binding currently exists with his champion, '[[Emile D'Arlan]]'.
