# Bullets
- Starting the session in [[Emberwood Village]], on December 16th. Downtime concluding.
- Begin with [[Spencer D. Kramer]], operating in some kind of laboratory working on a strange device.
- Some members of the [[Falling Fire]] seen around Emberwood Village earlier in the day, imploring people gather at [[Hendrix Farm]] away from the village per the words of [[Lucretia Mathias]]. 
- [[Emile D'Arlan]] then began the session, kicking things off with his research board.
	- At the [[Old Watchtower]], working amidst the Hooded Lanterns, he operated until exhaustion and fell asleep into a deep rest. This was a prelude before he fell into a small vision.
	- Encountering the Pilgrim under Stars, [[L'étranger]]. The Great Spirit entreated Emile in the midst of what would be a diverging incident of fate. An offer was made for a pact.
	- Things ended with a new Champion of Night named, a sword drawn in the name of laying low the proud and restoring balance between this world with the world of spirits, and a startled wake in the Old Watchtower before the day began.
- [[Levi SlaughterDale]] on his way back from providing assistance to the [[Hooded Lanterns]], getting advice from [[Antoine]] on where to retrieve a dog for further patrols in the Outer City.
	- The intermittent patrols that he was making were interrupted by [[Lieutenant Petra Lang]] also accompanied by dispatch leader, [[Antoine]]. Lieutenant Lang attempted to scout Levi for the Hooded Lanterns and they swapped stories of things from the Outer City.
	- He also took some time to go visit [[Oscar Yoren]], getting to interact with the [[Malfeasant]] mage to begin some kind of partnership in favor of the [[Queen's Men]]. Oscar Yoren heard out Levi and made a brief attempted to seize him when Levi introduced himself as 'the Anon'.
	- A deeper conversation was requested inside of [[Reed Manor]], though Levi thought better of going in alone despite Oscar Yoren being willing to hear him out.
	- He also saw [[Trunk Monkey]].
- [[Doctor Archivel Gecker Mobius]] kicked things off by finishing up a last week, learning new detailed from [[The Amethyst Academy]] regarding [[Delerium]], magic synthesis, and the variable properties of the material.
	- Learning different aspects of theory regarding delerium, Mobius is able to come to an understanding regarding certain properties that the magic stones have- which fly right in the face of standard defining rules of physics: such as being able to create or destroy matter, and being a perfectly efficient converter of energy with nearly none lost during the conversion process.
	- After taking Magic Synthesis lessons alongside [[River]], Mobius cleared all of the difficulty checks with flying colors, and earned himself a limited use prosthetic augment that takes a form close to a single-eye vision enhancer.
	- From here, he began debating what to do regarding the unique [[Ring of Ascension]] item that he had made for Thalia.
- [[Rupert McNamara]] concluded remaining time in the [[Chapel of Saint Ardenna]], speaking with [[Austin Edwards]] and [[Dame Claudia]] regarding the speech that he had given on temptation after that fateful conversation he had earlier in the week with Spencer D. Kramer.
	- It's during this time that Rupert begins gaining social traction within the community of Emberwood, many people coming to speak with him, learn his name, and receive some measure of respect from other members that are prominent within the community, such as [[Flamekeeper Isaac]] and [[High Flamekeeper Ophelia Reed]].
	- A courier approaches him near the end of this downtime period with a letter from the McNamara family. Detailing that today, December 16th, being his date of birth means they've sent a copy of a portrait from their manor of the late [[Sariah McNamara]]. Rupert proceeds to collect this piece and then deposit it within his temporary boarding at the Chapel.
- [[Thalia Bleakheart]] began her part of the session alongside [[Syr Baldrum]], participating in some short training and even venturing a short distance outside of Emberwood to what pass for woods nearby- a dead collection of trees far away enough from powerlines to be quiet.
	- Both of them took some time out here and there was some type of effort made to meditate, resulting in a very fitting natural one roll and a brief manifestation of small spirits around Thalia. Baldrum questioned this without receiving an explanation he could understand, before the two began heading back to the village.
- **At 2242, 10:42 PM on a moonless night, the Emberwood Incident is first observed by Levi SlaughterDale.**
	- He was in the middle of returning from an Outer City patrol as [[Erik 'Quick' 'Sexy' 'Hungry' Romero]].
- Levi radios in to Emile D'Arlan to attempt getting some awareness of the situation.
- At 10:43 PM, multiple sources of [[Witchfire]] visible in the Outer City coalesce into a massive ball of flame that launches itself from its point of origin directly to the opposite side of Emberwood. Mass amounts of [[Husks]] began turning in the direction of the village- many other creatures of land and sky starting in the direction of the witchfire.
- At 10:44, the first portals began opening to let the monsters of Drakkenheim cross the distance between them and Emberwood Village in an instance.
- At 10:45, [[Spencer D. Kramer]] makes an announcement and gets Rupert to spring to action to ring the bell at the Chapel of Saint Ardenna to wake the people of Emberwood Village.
- Emile D'Arlan sprints towards the abode of [[James 'Ocky' Ockman]] in order to make use of the radio system with Doctor Mobius, adding context for so many waking up in confusion and letting many rally to some amount of safety
- At 10:51, the factions mobilize.
	- [[The Amethyst Academy]] has their lead, [[River]], retreat as the [[Red Lion Hotel]] is turned from dwelling into defensible location by knights. Her aim becomes to secure the teleportation circle in [[Eventide Manor]], retreat, and get whatever support [[Archwizard Eldrick Runeweaver]] can provide to the village.
	- [[Falling Fire]] members begin calling forth people from Emberwood to run away in the direction of the farm, [[Nathaniel Flint]] traveling into Emberwood to provide assistance as others escape.
	- With the Old Watchtower of the [[Hooded Lanterns]] under siege, [[Lieutenant Petra Lang]] and [[Captain Ansom Lang]] call forth action and get [[Lord Commander Elias Drexel]] on the radio to request additional support as their stronghold goes under siege by [[Boog]], [[Sair]], and a number of other husks.
	- [[Knights of the Silver Order]] mount up on griffons, [[Knight-Captain Theodore Marshal]] visible in the distance alongside a number of other knights wielding weapons constructed by their artificers- thinning the tide that comes to crash against the village. Knight Captain specifically beginning to duel against a giant creature to prevent it from getting into Emberwood.
	- [[Queen's Men]] smugglers emerge from several sewer entrances, chased out by [[Husks]] and other creatures from Drakkenheim in pursuit. They quickly turn their efforts to relieving the villagers of their troubles in exchange for some coin, with [[Kuromi]] visible among the group of them making an effort to coordinate.
- The party takes a moment to gather among themselves and then mobilizes to nearby locations.
	- Thalia and Rupert first go towards the [[Red Lion Hotel]], told by [[Syr Baldrum]] that support was needed elsewhere. So the duo race towards the Chapel, encountering a creature known as a Wisp Liege and doing battle with many of the Will o' Wisps that it kept summoning- frightening away the child-like creature from the outside of the Chapel and assisting with the husks that take the Wisp Liege's place.
	- [[Erin Marlowe]] got visited by Levi very quickly, who provided her with the scroll of Rope Trick that he picked up from River several sessions ago.
	- D'Arlan, Levi, and Mobius hurry towards the Old Watchtower and see Captain Ansom unconscious and being carried away by Boog and Sair. With a dozen husks throwing contaminated spines at them as they attempt to help, Levi's able to distract with a well-timed illusion beside a shot from D'Arlan's [[Mk. II 'Hamstring']], Mobius hurrying some distance away before placing down [[Nosferatu]] and promptly feeding that goat three chips of [[Delerium]]. Luckily, through quick thinking and struggle, he was able to aim an Arcane Anomaly in the direction of Sair. Although it resisted the ensuing Hypnotic Pattern spell that the contaminated Nosferatu blasted towards it, the dregs that it was going to reveal hidden in the building it had landed on were affected and taken out swiftly.
	- Amidst the attacks, Kramer made another announcement, displaying [[Hosh]] unconscious from the top of a flying organic biplane. He declared that a mageborn in Emberwood was guilty of laying magic over this member of the clergy, before dropping Hosh from the plane into Emberwood during the battles.
	- Sair proceeded to fly away with Boog and Captain Ansom but D'Arlan was able to split off in pursuit, while Mobius and Levi chased towards the nearby runway where planes were struggling to take off. Levi dashed to the top of the warehouse the planes were in after seeing [[Amiri]] and [[Biscuit]] flying with a few planes overhead to provide air support. He stayed in the plane to provide more air support while Mobius helped fix planes and D'Arlan took [[Jessica]] in pursuit of the villains that took Captain Ansom, managing to free him from captivity in the middle of pinched battle thanks to some support and covering fire other Hooded Lanterns provided.
	- On the ride back to the Old Watchtower, Inspector D'Arlan notices a blinking kind of device on the side of Jessica. Captain Ansom tucked and rolled off the side while D'Arlan made the choice to steer the bike in the direction of some enemies before bailing, only to find the explosive was not an explosive at all! A portal opening in the last chiming sounds of the device, Jessica vanishing to some parts unknown.
- At 12:08 AM, once able to convene and speak over the radio with each other, the Ratcatchers saw which groups in Emberwood Village needed aid and turned their attention towards them now. It was only after a moment of deliberation on where to go, and if they could try to quell this attack at the source that they chose to assist the people of Emberwood instead through the powers that be. At this same time, Syr Baldrum draws what would be his last breath if not for sudden aid from Thalia Bleakheart before even a minute had gone by.
	- While rushing to new locations or keeping other areas safe, the party witnessed Hooded Lanterns firing on Queen's Men in the streets as the thieves made attempts to help civilians into boarded up houses in exchange for loosening their grip on their coin purses, saw [[Aldor the Immense]] keeping in business to supply munitions during the attack, and lightning crackle from above Hendrix Farm in the distance as a prelude to rain that snuffed many building fires on the buildings in town.
	- Levi kept up in the sky to await potential support to where it might be needed from the two duos, Rupert and Thalia remaining at the Red Lion Hotel while D'Arlan and Mobius moved towards [[Eventide Manor]] after seeing the shield guardian of River guarding the exterior, as well as some lights inside. When the Manor was neared, there was some commotion that drew Levi's presence and he headed in that direction as mass amounts of other creatures descend onto the building.
- At 12:14 AM, minutes into the new day, James Ockman dies. His last moments were surrounded by few friends, with a last request to Doctor Mobius to keep an eye on [[James Jr.]]
- Defense of the Red Lion Hotel and Eventide Manor begins in new swinging hours that go by until sun rises.
	- Hours pass by. The fight goes on.
	- D'Arlan, Levi, and Mobius encounter ally [[Mageborn]] from the Amethyst Academy ready for combat. They introduce the helpful trio to some of the [[Splobiaba]] before needing to defend against dangerous [[Deep Dregs]]. James Junior proceeds to offer resolute assistance in the defense of the dwelling used by the Academy.
	- Kramer made another announcement as the groups kept strong, shouting that D'Arlan was responsible for aiding an infiltration of the Hooded Lanterns. Taunting yet again before flying away.
	- Rupert and Thalia are able to deter a number of lycanthropes from the Red Lion Hotel as the Knights of the Silver Order establish themselves. A brief conflict between a number of the knights and Nathaniel Flint is witnessed, quelled momentarily by [[High Flamekeeper Ophelia Reed]].
- At 5:45 AM, the last remaining monsters are slain or flee back in the direction of Drakkenheim. Spencer D. Kramer is sighted departing, having made his closing exit and lasting impact on Emberwood.
- At 6:01 AM, Emile D'Arlan requisitions a plane and a full jerry can of gasoline, chasing after Kramer as the sun rises. Rupert watches him go and gives last regards over the radio, Thalia keeps silent, Levi gives a grinning farewell, and Mobius shares a wave while smoking his pipe.
