# Bullets
- Starting the session in [[Emberwood Village]], on December 1st. No Nut November has concluded and Destroy Delerium December begins. Yippee.
	- Big start occurred when a cannon sounded off the first day of the [[Dawn Duels]], with star reporter [[Felix Firecracker]] recounting the latest adventuring group in [[Emberwood Village]]- the Ratcatchers!
		- [[Emile D'Arlan]] denied comment on any large issues or his romantic life as Emberwood's Most-Eligible Bachelor when questioned by the reporter.
		- [[Levi SlaughterDale]] begins on the way to have a conversation with [[River]], regarding a ring from [[The Amethyst Academy]] and an examination for his [[Contamination]].
		- [[Doctor Archivel Gecker Mobius]] obliged a grand plan for the new year of advancement, support for others in the town, and created a good public foothold for some of the Academy. This was matched with some publicity raked in from the events of the fireworks from the [[Feast of the Faithful]].
		- [[Rupert McNamara]] remained silent that didn't make it on air, with the reporter making a few barbs regarding his late wife along the way.
		- [[Thalia Bleakheart]] played the diligent part of a wall, with some embellishment from the reporter on her exploits in the city.
- Downtime begins, five days down of fifteen.
- Thalia: 'Dawn Duels and recovery.'
	- Faced off in the dawn duels against a number of opponents, including [[Brom the Brash]] and [[Edelgard Krieg]] in a grand series of bouts. She secured victory over both of them and many others, earning a hot streak in the midst of a larger gathering during the year. She and Rupert both after winning the majority of their bouts secured a valuable armband crafter by makers from the [[Knights of the Silver Order]], but also a short audience with [[High Flamekeeper Ophelia Reed]].
	- Between the duels, she also was beseeched by an attendant from the [[Von Kessel Colosseum]], which offered a vial of a booster agent called 'Titansurge' that Rupert recognized. She turned it down.
	- Additionally, she visited Emberwood Village, returning from her trip with a unique new power in her arsenal.
	- Dubbed 'the Ashen Angel' by [[Felix Firecracker]].
- Rupert: 'Husks and Hazing.'
	- Traveled into [[Drakkenheim]] to make it to the McNamara Estate in the Outer City, seeking to recover the deed to his house with the help of Levi, and doing so- as well as recovering a Brassard made by his wife from long years ago.
	- Retrieved the deed to his house and secured funding for Plate Armor from [[Jason 'the Coin Flipper' Teller]], reminding the greedy capitalist that if he dared force improper rates on individuals that the oath Rupert took to slay evil might turn towards the direction of the loan-keeper. A good negotiation ensued and a proper rate was offered for the armor that [[Tobias Crowe]] set into crafting for Rupert- to be finished around the tenth of the month.
	- After coming back from this task, he joined Thalia in some light training at the Dawn Duels. Managing to secure victory over both [[Mirarda]] and [[Brom the Brash]], though meeting his match with the Coffin Crafter, Edelgard Krieg, who got one over on him. He and Thalia both after winning the majority of their bouts secured a valuable armband crafter by makers from the [[Knights of the Silver Order]], but also a short audience with [[High Flamekeeper Ophelia Reed]].
	- Dubbed 'the Contaminated Crusader' by [[Felix Firecracker]] and [[Officer Yannick]].
- Dr. Mobius: 'Research, he remarked.'
	- Mobius worked out some Academy-sponsored lectures, focusing Experimental Weaponry to start and struggling with some of the material taught by Remark, once known as the wizard [[Abjection]]. Both spoke for a time, to which some information was shared by Remark that revealed [[Spencer D. Kramer]] would be the source of recent research that while not fully uncovered by him will be credited as the original father of some great advancement in magic that, quote, 'would get a significant amount of attention towards [[The Amethyst Academy]], welcome or otherwise'. Mobius lamented that he felt he was always behind Kramer, managing to get a few words between himself and Levi in between attempts at his craft- working on some infusions.
- Levi: 'A hand in every pie.'
	- Providing some crucial assistance, Levi was there for Rupert in visiting the McNamara estate- disguising himself as a husk and managing to wade inside amongst the Husks that were taking up residence. He acquired the deed with little struggle, as well as an item that was touched by the [[Spirits]], specifically one of a fallen friend from Rupert's past.
	- Under the monitoring and assessment of [[River]], successfully took the first step and became a journeyman mage of [[The Amethyst Academy]]- earning a first academy ring.
- Emile:
	- Secured information regarding Kramer from Tobias Crowe.
	- Began scouting the [[Midnight Court]] as his alternate identity, Camilio Petri, and encountering a member of the [[Queen's Men]] by the name of Dmitri who was willing to get Camilio a lay of the land before hearing 'new members need better vetting' from [[Jupiter Jack]]. 
	- Foiled in the first attempt to gain footing, Camilio then found [[Miss Bliss]] willing to establish a point of contact for him within the criminal underbelly of Emberwood Village.
	- Shy of meeting with [[Lieutenant Petra Lang]], Emile did receive a military packset from the lieutenant of the Hooded Lanterns.
