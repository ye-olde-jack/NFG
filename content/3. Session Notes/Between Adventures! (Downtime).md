Downtime in Drakkenheim is best described as allocating days into specific activities. This can be spent in just about any way, and below are a number of different means that anyone can attempt to use in order to advance themselves via factions.

**Important details for Downtime! Remember R.O.C:**
- **Resources.** Each downtime activity includes a resources section that outlines the time, gold, or other commitments characters must dedicate to participate. As the player, you’ll need to ensure these costs are clear and appropriate for the activity’s scope alongside the GM. Faction Activities typically span a workweek of 5 days, with resource requirements reflecting the difficulty or exclusivity of what the characters are attempting.
- **Outcomes.** Every downtime activity when selected will have a roll associated with the end of the time spent. Success will lead to some advancement in the given group, but also will receive some potential notice from other factions as well- while Failure will lead to a negative and also a greater notice from other factions. This encourages specialization between one or two groups, though if you're the type to want to spread yourself thin then this is where your fellow party members come into play- rely on one another to build up group relationships.
	- Loss of points in faction as a result of individual play is intentional. It's to show that factions cannot have their problems or bridges between each other built by one person alone. Teamwork makes the dream work is true in this option of downtime.
- **Complications.** Interacting with factions can sometimes incur sudden or unexpected complications. These can range from minor inconveniences to more serious consequences, such as strained relationships or mechanical penalties.

- Attending a Ceremony of Faith. [[Falling Fire]] or [[Knights of the Silver Order]] activity.
	The two groups offers protection against [[Contamination]] for those who participate in its sacred rites and ceremonies. This downtime activity involves daily prayers, blessings, and rituals that are believed to shield congregants from the darkness that encroaches upon the city.
	- **Resources:** Participating in these rites requires a donation of 5 gp per day spent within the [[Chapel of Saint Ardenna]] to one of the given groups, reflecting offerings, special vestments, and ritual supplies. The character must commit at least 8 hours each day to prayer, meditation, and ceremonial duties to gain the benefits.
- Carousing in the Midnight Court Black Market. [[Hooded Lanterns]] or [[Queen's Men]] activity.
	Emberwood Village's Black Market is a covert labyrinth of forbidden indulgences, where opulent dens offer a dizzying array of substances, mind-altering elixirs, and strange alchemical concoctions. In these dark corners of the city, secrets are traded alongside illicit pleasures, and characters can mingle with criminals, informants, and other curious patrons of the fallen city’s underworld now settling in a nearby village. For those willing to risk the side effects and associations, carousing in these dens can yield valuable information, temporary alliances, and insight into the city’s hidden workings.
	- **Resources:** Carousing in the Midnight Court dens requires 10 gp per day, spent on entry fees, drinks, drugs, and occasional bribes to keep unsavory company at bay. The character spends five days indulging, during which they engage with various patrons and take part in the dens’ strange offerings.

- Fight in the [[Dawn Duels]]. A [[Knights of the Silver Order]] activity innately, though could benefit other groups as well.
	The improvised colosseum in [[Camp Dawn]] offers a chance for fighters from all walks of life near [[Emberwood Village]] to come to test their mettle before an audience. Open monthly to the public, these brutal spectacles pit combatants sometimes against monstrous beasts from the countryside away from [[Drakkenheim]] or skilled warriors in an arena soaked in blood and intrigue. Sponsored by the Elyrian Church and their noble elite, these events promise fame, fortune, and potential favor from powerful figures. Yet, the risks are severe—not just from the physical battles but also from the machinations of nobles of fallen Drakkenheim, [[Elyria]], and the colosseum’s shadowy underbelly.
	- **Resources:** To participate in the improvised colosseum, characters must pay an entry fee of 10 gp plus 10 gp for each tier above the entry tier (of which there are three). The fee grants entry to three fights and covers access to the arena, minimal medical aid, and basic gear. Competitors must dedicate five days to preparation and combat. Between fights, they gather intelligence on their opponents, repair equipment, or recover from injuries.
- Patrol with local Militia. [[Hooded Lanterns]] or [[Knights of the Silver Order]] activity.
	The Militia of the [[Hooded Lanterns]] and the [[Knights of the Silver Order]] keeps [[Emberwood Village]]'s streets guarded against the nightly encroachments of [[Contamination]]. Characters who join these patrols face encounters with mutated beasts, infiltrating horrors, and the ever-present threat of madness that lurks under the city’s corrupted streets. Assisting the militia in this way allows characters to earn respect, a modest wage, and potential connections within the above-mentioned factions. However, these patrols are not without risk, and even seasoned militia members must steel themselves for the dangers of the night as well as make peace with protecting villagers from potentially hidden contaminants brought about by refugees.
	- **Resources:** Patrols require each character to spend five days assisting the Militia of the Silver Order or Hooded Lanterns. No specific resources are required beyond the character’s equipment, but they must be prepared to face dangerous encounters in unpredictable circumstances.
- Rescue Refugees or lost pilgrims. [[Falling Fire]] or [[Knights of the Silver Order]] activity.
	The city of [[Drakkenheim]] has been cautioned away from passage by the Knight of the Silver Order due to its proximity to outbreaks of [[Contamination]]. The city is a place of desperation for many who remain trapped within its boundaries for one reason or another, with members of the [[Falling Fire]] and even some members from the [[Knights of the Silver Order]] seemingly taking a stance against the Church. Success may earn the party the trust of the above-mentioned factions and the gratitude of those they save, but it also risks putting them at odds with the Church.
	- **Resources:** This activity requires a 10 gp fee per character to cover bribes, forged documents, safehouse fees, and basic supplies for the refugees. Characters must also dedicate five days to the operation, potentially coordinating with faction contacts, mapping safe routes, and preparing for potential encounters with Hooded Lantern Militia patrols.
- Study in an Academy-sponsored lecture. For [[The Amethyst Academy]] related activity.
	[[The Amethyst Academy]] periodically hold open lectures for those who wish to expand their understanding of the scientific and alchemical arts. These sessions cover advanced, often unsettling topics—from anatomical experimentation to the hybridization of living and mechanical elements. Participants brave enough to attend these lectures gain a glimpse into cutting-edge theories and techniques, but the material is rigorous and not without personal risk.
	- **Resources:** Attending an Academy lecture requires a 10 gp fee, covering entry, lecture materials, and minor equipment for demonstrations. Characters must commit five days to the activity, attending sessions and performing guided study under instruction. 
	- **Lecture Options:** These include a ==subject addressed (then skills influenced)== as the format. Subjects include Morphic Alchemy (Arcana & Nature), Magic Synthesis (Intelligence & Sleight of Hand), Prosthetic Adaptation (Medicine & Athletics), [[Delerium]]'s Biology (Nature & Perception), Experimental Weaponry (Sleight of Hand & Athletics), and lastly, Mental Fortitude Technique (Insight & Intimidation).
	- **Special Note:** This activity requires two rolls, succeeding on both means that other tasks can be done in addition to lectures- including though not limited to training with others, making magic items, and carousing.
- Volunteer with local healers. [[Falling Fire]] activity.
	The Chapel of Saint Ardenna is filled to capacity and has few healers, with many going to [[Hendrix Farm]] as citizens suffering from Contamination attacks, accidents, and other afflictions. Volunteers from all factions and walks of life provide crucial support to the overwhelmed staff, assisting with treatments, managing supplies, and providing comfort to the distressed. Characters who lend their skills to these hospitals gain unique experience, gratitude, and possible connections with all factions.
	- **Resources**: Requires no monetary cost, but characters must dedicate five days to healing work, following the lead of staff and aiding in a wide range of tasks. Volunteering at the [[Hendrix Farm]] offers unique challenges for those experienced in dealing with [[Contamination]]-related issues.
