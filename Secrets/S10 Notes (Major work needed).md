# DM Token Uses:
- **1 Point:**
	- "Field Promotion" to make a powerful creature legendary or increase weak creature's CR by 2, 3, and 4- the weaker the creature the greater the bump.
	- "Twist of Fate" to break a DM token into 5 player tokens.
- **2 Point:**
	- "Field Promotion - Greater" to make a decently strong creature mythic or an already powerful creature Legendary. Already Legendary creatures gain new things.
- **3 Point:**
	- "A Living, Breathing World" to add in some type of facet to the environment not previously there which complicates things for the party and adds favor to the enemies if they're present.
- **4/5 Point:**
	- **4P** - Cooldown of 5 sessions but can apply to multiple characters across the session: "Stroke of Fortune" for changing lethality on player characters to make them captured, caught, or otherwise believed to be dead and otherwise. This can be used during a combat and the fight gets harder, but any near dead PCs stop making death saves. ==Surrender should be made available by this point.==
	- **5P** - Cooldown of 7/10 sessions: "Trade of Tragedy" make a devil's bargain and request all player character's tokens in exchange for having post death adventure or similar to get party alive once again. Something happens in the world as a result of them all dying, likely the passage of time leading to a series of very bad events. ==Use of this ability might lead to session immediately swapping from play to talking.==
